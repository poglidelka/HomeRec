## What is HomeRec

HomeRec is a free, non-commercial revival of Rec Room's 2026 era. It runs the original 2026
game client against a server we rebuilt ourselves. The game plays the way it did in 2026, and it
has a few things you won't find anywhere else.

This is not an old build or a remake. It is the actual 2026 client, with the 2026 Rec Center,
the Watch menu and the 2026 store.

## Features

| | |
|---|---|
| 🏠 **The real 2026 game** | The 2026 Rec Center, the Watch menu and the 2026 store. |
| 🧱 **Rooms 2.0 and Room AI** | Build in Rooms 2.0, and let Room AI place, paint and move objects from a simple text message. Room AI is still in testing. |
| 🤖 **Roomie** | Your AI companion talks back by voice or text, in English and Russian. |
| 💰 **Creators keep 100%** | Sell rooms, keys and items. Every token goes to the creator and co-owners, with no platform cut. |
| 🎁 **Play and earn** | Tokens and daily gifts for playing, XP for time spent in game, and daily challenges. |
| 🎉 **Friends, parties and minigames** | Voice chat, parties and the built-in minigames: Floor is Lava, Hide and Seek and Snowball Fight. |
| 🌐 **Your account on the web** | Sign in on [homerec.net](https://homerec.net) with your game account to browse players' photos and profiles, and give hearts (the same cheers as in game). |
| 🥽 **VR headset sign-in** | Sign in once on your headset and it remembers you. |

## How to play

1. **Create an account** at [homerec.net](https://homerec.net/signup), or sign in with the one you
   already play with.
2. **Download the launcher** for Windows PC from [homerec.net](https://homerec.net). It sets up
   everything you need.
3. **Join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/XJ5hAEg6rE)** for news, help and beta access.

> HomeRec is in **closed beta** and spots are limited. Ask in Discord to get in.

## FAQ

**Is this the official Rec Room?**
No. HomeRec is an independent fan project and has no connection to Rec Room Inc.

**Does it cost anything?**
No. HomeRec is free and non-commercial. Tokens are earned by playing, never sold for real money.

**Can I use my old Rec Room account?**
No. HomeRec runs its own server, so you create a new account here.

**Is the server code open source?**
Not at this time.
